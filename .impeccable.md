## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is a non-technical macOS user creating a personal digital human for natural, knowledge-grounded conversation. They should never need to understand model processes, ports, YAML, SSH tunnels, vector databases, or provider-specific protocol details.

The core job is to turn one portrait, a short voice sample, and selected knowledge into a digital human that can answer a real question as quickly and confidently as possible.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, alive. The product should feel like Apple-style invisible technology with the emotional warmth of a private creative studio.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined native macOS composition with generous space, restrained color, tactile media moments, and quiet motion that suggests presence rather than spectacle. Avoid technical dashboards, neon AI styling, glass-heavy card grids, chatbot clichés, and configuration-wizard aesthetics.

### Design Principles

1. Prepare before experience: complete and verify required technology before creation, while keeping technical configuration product-owned and understandable.
2. Ask only for human inputs: portrait, voice, knowledge, and intent; infer technical choices automatically.
3. Present preparation as human capabilities and outcomes, not models, endpoints, ports, or infrastructure.
4. Preserve user control through clear privacy routing, skip paths, undo, and graceful degradation.
5. Make waiting feel like creation: show the digital human taking shape while setup and downloads run in parallel.
